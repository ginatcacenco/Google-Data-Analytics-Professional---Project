🏢 Real Estate Market Analysis - Iași (2021–2025)
📊 Project Overview
This data analysis project investigates the evolution of residential real estate prices in Iași, Romania, from 2021 through 2025. It correlates apartment square-meter prices with key macroeconomic indicators—specifically net average wages, inflation rates, and the EUR/RON exchange rate—to understand market affordability and growth dynamics.

Author: Gina Ioana Tcacenco

Interactive Dashboard: Tableau Public Profile – Gina Ioana Tcacenco

GitHub Repository: ginatcacenco

📈 Key Statistics & Findings (2021 - 2025)
Year	Average Price (€ / sqm)	Price Growth (%)	Net Average Salary (RON)	Net Average Salary (EUR)	Inflation Rate (%)

2022	€1,326	+3.03%	3,973.8 RON	€805.8	13.8%
2023	€1,408	+6.18%	4,584.2 RON	€926.8	10.4%
2024	€1,602	+13.78%	5,196.7 RON	€1,044.6	5.6%
2025	€1,837	+14.67%	5,536.0 RON	€1,097.7	7.3%
Upward Trajectory: Property prices in Iași experienced accelerated growth, jumping from 1,287 €/sqm in 2021 to 1,837 €/sqm by 2025 (a cumulative increase of over 42%).

Wage vs. Real Estate Growth: While net average monthly wages grew significantly from 3,526.5 RON in 2021 to 5,536 RON in 2025, real estate price spikes (especially in 2024 at +13.78% and 2025 at +14.67%) outpaced general wage growth, impacting housing affordability.

Macroeconomic Pressures: High inflation peaks in 2022 (13.8%) and 2023 (10.4%) directly influenced construction costs, currency shifts, and pricing adjustments across the local market.
